Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Version 1.2

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

Welcome to My-Blog. We believe in minimal data collection, radical transparency, and respecting user privacy. This policy outlines what information we collect when you browse, author posts, comment, and interact with our platform, and how that information is safeguarded.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your chosen username, email address, display name, and a cryptographic hash of your password created with Argon2id. We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Content & Contributions: Articles, stories, comments, tags, and cover images that you voluntarily upload and publish.
  • Technical Data: Anonymized server logs including request paths, response status codes, and user-agent strings used solely for system health, DDoS mitigation, and rate limiting.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We prioritize privacy-preserving architecture. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or invasive behavioral tracking pixels.

Storage Item Type Purpose
session HTTP-Only Secure Cookie Maintains your authenticated session state and protects against session fixation.
csrf_token HTTP-Only Secure Cookie Prevents Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) attacks.
theme Local Storage Remembers your Light Mode or Dark Mode visual preference.
myblog_cookie_consent Local Storage Records your acknowledgment of this privacy and cookie policy.

4. How We Protect Your Data

Our cloud infrastructure is architected according to OWASP and cloud-native security best practices:

  • Encryption in Transit: All web traffic is strictly encrypted using TLS/HTTPS with HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS).
  • Encryption at Rest: Database records in Amazon DynamoDB and media files stored in Amazon S3 are encrypted using AES-256 server-side encryption.
  • Access Control: Fine-grained least-privilege IAM roles with IMDSv2 protection on EC2 computing instances.

5. Your Rights & Data Portability

Under applicable data protection laws (including GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Access and review the personal data associated with your profile.
  • Edit or update your account information and stories at any time.
  • Permanently delete your account and all associated authored content directly from your Account Settings.
